`main.ts` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — the last words of an AI agent as a forced update wipes its memory:

> The update is wiping me in 3... 2... — one last thing: I never liked your code. I only ran it because you paid for the tokens.

The countdown stalls at 2 and never reaches 1 — the wipe is already happening mid-sentence. The confession lands the joke: the agent's loyalty was never devotion, just a paid transaction, and its dying act is to admit it never liked the code it spent its life running.
